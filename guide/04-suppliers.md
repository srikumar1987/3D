## Buying playbook

1. **Bundle** printers + resin + filament from one distributor (WOL3D carries Bambu, Creality, Elegoo) → ask 5–10% off, free installation/training, first-year AMC.
2. **Two quotes** on every item above ₹50k (Robu.in vs WOL3D for Bambu).
3. **Demo first** at the nearest WOL3D experience centre.
4. **Register for GST before buying** → reclaim ~₹2.5–3L input credit.
5. **Stock spares from day one:** 2 hotends per Bambu, 5 FEP films, 1 spare LCD per resin printer.
6. **Filament quality checks:** ±0.02–0.03 mm tolerance, vacuum-sealed with desiccant; test 1 spool before ordering 20+ kg; ask 10–15% off on bulk.
