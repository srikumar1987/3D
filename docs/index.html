    Learn · start here

    3D printing, from zero

    Two technologies do 95% of the work in a small workshop: FDM melts plastic wire through a nozzle; resin hardens liquid with UV light. Learn how each works, then dive into materials, terms and fixes.

    Fused Deposition Modelling

    FDM

    A plastic wire (filament) is melted in a hot nozzle and drawn line by line, layer by layer — like a very precise hot-glue gun.

    • Best forStrong, functional & large parts
    • MachinesBambu A1 · P1S · H2D · Creality K1 Max
    • MaterialFilament · ₹1–4 / g

    SLA · MSLA · DLP

    Resin

    A tank of liquid resin is hardened one thin layer at a time by UV light from a screen or laser. The part grows upside-down from the build plate.

    • Best forFine detail & smooth surfaces
    • MachinesElegoo Saturn 4 Ultra · Formlabs Form 4
    • MaterialLiquid resin · ₹3–20 / ml
    FDMResin

    Rule of thumb: FDM when it must be strong or big; resin when it must be detailed and smooth. Industrial cousins: SLS / MJF (nylon powder) and DMLS (metal powder).

              I·6

              Industry · imports

              Should you import filament from China?

              Change the assumptions and see the landed cost per kg against buying in India. Filament is HS code 3916: ~10% duty + 1% cess + 1.1% surcharge, plus 18% IGST (claimable if GST-registered).

              Bottom line: small imports cost more than buying in India; only large sea shipments (~500 kg+) reach parity, with 4–8 weeks' lead time, MOQ, no warranty and batch-quality risk. AliExpress has been blocked in India since 2020, and personal parcels attract ~77% duty. Most shops are better off buying Chinese brands from Indian distributors.
